Gakusen Toshi Asterisk Fanservice Review – Episodes 11-12

OVERALL

Here,Some Fanservice Compilation-y thing for you guys…Fanservice highlights of the first 12 episodes:

Gakusen Toshi Asterisk was a good anime… It was nothing special and added nothing to the genre but it was not bad either and can only get better considering it actually got a second cour unlike most other LN harem/battle anime… Also i must mention Gakusen Toshi Asterisk had the best art/animation compared to other shows of this genre we saw recently(Taimadou Gakuen 35 Shiken Shoutai,Kuusen Madoushi Kouhosei no Kyoukan,Absolute Duo,Seiken Tsukai no World Break,Trinity Seven)… It had so much potential but imo two main characters : Ayato and Julis are the main flaw of this series… They are simply boring, they do not add something to the plot or the PLOT… Wouldn’t mind watching a “Urzaiz sisters” or “Saya x Kirin spinoff” instead…

 

Characters:

Main duo was extremely generic and boring… I liked all of the side characters though, especially Kirin and Claudia…

Plot/Setting:

Magical children going to magical academy to do magic battles… And main characters joins the tournament to win against other evil academies… Thats it, the whole plot of the first cour…

Art/Animation:

A-1 Pictures did a great job… Everything looks great : Characters,Fights,Backgrounds… Best looking magic/harem/battle of the last years… Or,was best until KyoAni decided to do an anime of this genre…

Fanservice:

Kirin and Claudia did their best to provide us fanservice… But Ayato is your usual harem MC so he didn’t want to help them for that cause… There was no change in the first BD as expected and probably won’t be any nudity in the future BDs either…

Overall:

Its a good anime of its genre with a great production values… Don’t expect too much from it and enjoy the fun ride…

My Best Girl is Kirin… Such sexiness and cuteness in one body is just too much… Even though Claudia was the sexiest girl she can’t compete with Kirin when you add the Kawaii factor…

Many thanks to ScissorMeTimbers for providing me stitch/webm support throughout this series, and you guys for reading the reviews… See you all again for the second cour of Gakusen Toshi Asterisk in Spring 2016…
