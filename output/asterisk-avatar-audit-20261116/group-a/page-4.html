[Review] Gakusen Toshi Asterisk 2nd Season

6/17/2016

Comments

 
Final moments of the last episode.
Gakusen Toshi Asterisk has finished its 2nd Season and from the looks of it, I'm hoping that they make a 3rd Season as well featuring the Gryps Festa. I'm going to be honest with this review though, at first I thought that this was your average ecchi-harem anime when the first season came out (which it definitely was and still is) but truth be told the plot was better than what I imagined it would be.
Ayato and Julis' teamwork on Episode 1.
Season 2 was not like the first season wherein the focus was more on the girls and the harem, instead they focused on the Phoenix Festa and the fight scenes which was great. There were still some ecchi scenes but not that much like the first season.
Li Shenyun's face looking at the panicked Ayato.
One particular episode, or episodes rather, that I found thrilling was when Ayato and Julis were up against Jie Long Seventh Institute's Li siblings Shenyun and Shenhua on episodes 3 and 4. Li Shenyun was good at playing the bad guy, which he was, but I think it was way too overboard. XD

This match was indeed one of the best in the whole series since it was in this match that Ayato was able to "unlock" his "potential". Prior to this match, it was only within 5 minutes that he can use his powers but after the events that had happened on this match he was able to extend this time limit to an hour, according to him, which then helped them win the match.
After defeating Li Shenhua, Ayato proceeds to demolish Li Shenyun (above)
Right after this match was the match between Seidoukan Academy's Sasamiya Saya and Toudou Kirin and the Mechs of Arlequint Academy, AR-D and RM-C. It was a match to watch as well, not just because I have a bias with Kirin, but because almost all battles with Kirin (and her swordsmanship) is just so much fun to watch. XD
Kirin breaking AR-D's barrier.
Kirin forces AR-D to attack before the 1 minute mark.
I've just shown you the first half of the series and it's already this much excitement. All-in-all, this was a very fun and exciting series to watch. And I'm still hoping that they would release a 3rd Season featuring the Gryps Festa wherein there will be 5 members in a team instead of a pair in the previous Festa.

That wraps up my short review of Gakusen Toshi Asterisk 2nd Season! Thanks for reading~ :D
