## 一輝

參考：`ikki-idle-v3.png`（主底稿）、`references/ikki-face-target.png`（臉部约束）。

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: transparent 2D game combat sprite sheet, 3 columns by 2 rows. Edit and extend the existing character illustration into EXACTLY SIX distinct full-body animation keyframes. Landscape 1536x1024 or 3072x2048 canvas, six equal square cells. Each entire character AND entire sword must stay inside its own square with a 7% empty gutter, no overlap or clipping. All six have identical camera angle, body scale, head size and planted foot baseline at local y=90%. Clean anime cel animation lineart, faithful to source colors, face and clothing construction. Genuinely transparent alpha everywhere except character and weapon. No aura, shadow, floor, scenery, text, borders or grid. Animation is physical limb articulation, NOT just rotating or translating a still image. Row major order: TOP LEFT frame0 WALK-A: a long advancing stride, left leg leading, right leg trailing. TOP MIDDLE frame1 WALK-B: passing step, knee lifted under the torso, supporting leg vertical. TOP RIGHT frame2 WALK-C: opposite stride, right leg leading, left leg trailing. Three walk frames must have clearly different leg silhouettes but consistent arms carrying weapon ready. BOTTOM LEFT frame3 WINDUP: both hands raise sword above and behind the shoulder, knees bent, preparing cut. BOTTOM MIDDLE frame4 SLASH: body lunges, arms extend, sword has physically swung down and forward, a clear powerful followthrough, no motion-blur smears. BOTTOM RIGHT frame5 GUARD: recovering balanced stance, sword held diagonally across chest. Keep original anatomically slender normal anime proportions, NOT chibi. Preserve reference identity rather than redesign. Image1 is the PRIMARY edit target: current Ikki Kurogane standing sprite. Image2 is a supporting official face closeup: preserve short gentle youthful chin, soft cheeks, gray eyes, long diagonal asymmetric black bangs and modest mouth. Match existing white black-piped school jacket, gray lapels, black undershirt and trousers, black boots, black katana Intetsu. All SIX faces and bodies are oriented 3/4 TOWARD SCREEN RIGHT, advancing right, looking right. Windup sword behind to left; slash blade forward to right. Preserve hair shape, jacket hem patterns and blade proportions across all frames.
